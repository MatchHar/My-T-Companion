# My T Companion 1.10.63

Adds optional Footprint Passport metadata from recorded trip endpoints: countries, provinces/states, cities, first/last recorded dates and distinct visiting drives. Saved endpoints do not identify every city crossed.

Passport mode omits trail geometry while retaining the same coverage cells and statistics. Older clients remain compatible.

Bounds trail retention, streams positions, preserves GPS discontinuities and reports omitted routes. No vehicle commands, notification changes or TeslaMate upgrade.
